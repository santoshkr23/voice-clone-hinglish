# Voice Clone Recording Script (about 10–12 min)

## Before you record
- **Room:** quiet, with the fan and AC off. A room with curtains, a bed or a wardrobe gives less echo than an empty room.
- **Mic:** the same mic you used for the VO, held at the **same distance** (about one hand-width from your mouth) for the whole session.
- **Level: record louder than last time.** Your loudest words should peak around **-6 dB** in Audacity (last time they were too quiet).
- **Format:** Audacity, **WAV, 48000 Hz, mono**. One file per section is easiest: `01_calm.wav`, `02_reel.wav`, and so on.
- **Gaps:** leave **1 second of silence** before and after every line.
- **Mistakes:** if you stumble, stay quiet for 2 seconds and **read the whole line again**. Don't stop recording; I'll cut the mistakes out like last time.
- **Delivery:** speak naturally, the way you talk in your vlogs. Don't do a "radio voice".
- **Session length:** do it in one sitting if you can, because your voice changes from day to day. Drink water first.

---

## 01 · Calm narration (documentary voice, slow, warm)
1. Aaj main aapko ek aisi jagah le chalta hoon, jahan waqt bhi dheere-dheere chalta hai.
2. Subah ke chhe baje the, aur shehar abhi poori tarah jaaga bhi nahi tha.
3. Khidki ke bahar door tak hare-bhare khet, aur unke peeche dhundh mein chhupe pahaad.
4. Kabhi-kabhi safar ka asli matlab manzil nahi, balki raasta hota hai.
5. Is gaon mein lagbhag do hazaar log rehte hain, aur sabki subah ek jaisi shuru hoti hai.
6. Yahan ki galiyan patli hain, ghar chhote hain, lekin logon ke dil bahut bade hain.
7. Main jab bhi kisi naye desh mein jaata hoon, sabse pehle wahan ka local khana try karta hoon.
8. Train dheere-dheere station se nikli, aur ek naya safar shuru ho gaya.
9. Is shehar ka itihaas paanch sau saal se bhi zyada purana hai.
10. Raat ho chuki thi, sadkon par sirf street lights aur kuch bikes ki awaaz thi.

## 02 · Reel hooks (energetic, fast, smiling while you speak)
1. Ruko ruko! Yeh video skip mat karna!
2. Sirf ₹500 mein poora din ghoomna? Haan, yeh possible hai!
3. India se sirf chaar ghante ki flight, aur aap ek bilkul alag duniya mein!
4. Yeh jagah dekh ke mera dimaag hi kharab ho gaya!
5. Ninety-nine percent log yeh galti karte hain jab woh Bali jaate hain.
6. Agar aap Indonesia jaa rahe ho, toh yeh teen cheezein zaroor jaan lo.
7. Bhai, itna saaf station maine life mein pehli baar dekha!
8. Chalo, aaj aapko dikhata hoon duniya ka sabse sasta street food.
9. Number three dekh ke aap shock ho jaoge!
10. Wait for it… yeh view dekhiye!

## 03 · Questions and curiosity (rising tone)
1. Kya aapne kabhi socha hai ki yeh log itne khush kaise rehte hain?
2. Aapko kya lagta hai, is ticket ki price kitni hogi?
3. Kya India mein bhi aisa ho sakta hai?
4. Aap is jagah jaana chahoge? Comment mein batao!
5. Lekin sabse bada sawaal… yeh sab hua kaise?
6. Kitne mein mila? Andaaza lagao!

## 04 · Excited and surprised (big reactions)
1. Wow! Yeh toh kamaal hai!
2. Arre baap re, itni bheed!
3. Maza aa gaya yaar, sach mein!
4. Yeh kya hai? Main toh hairan hoon!
5. Oh my God, look at that view!
6. Haha, yeh toh maine expect hi nahi kiya tha!

## 05 · Soft, emotional, serious (quiet, slow)
1. Kuch jagahen aapko badal deti hain, chupchaap, bina kuch kahe.
2. Ghar se door rehkar hi ghar ki asli keemat samajh aati hai.
3. Us din mujhe samajh aaya ki khushi kitni chhoti cheezon mein hoti hai.
4. Shukriya, is safar mein mere saath rehne ke liye.

## 06 · Numbers, prices, times (clear, normal pace)
1. Ticket sirf pachaas hazaar rupiah ka tha, yaani lagbhag do sau saath rupaye.
2. Flight subah saat bajkar pandrah minute par thi.
3. Hotel ka kiraya ek raat ka bas nau sau ninyaanve rupaye.
4. Humne teen ghante mein bees kilometre cover kiye.
5. Twenty twenty-five mein maine paanch desh ghoome.
6. Ek, do, teen, chaar, paanch, chhe, saat, aath, nau, das.

## 07 · English lines (your natural English accent)
1. Welcome back to the channel, and today we're going somewhere special.
2. This is one of the most beautiful places I have ever visited.
3. The train was clean, on time, and surprisingly comfortable.
4. Let me know in the comments where I should go next.
5. Honestly, I did not expect this at all.

## 08 · Places and travel words (say each clearly, a 1 sec gap between words)
Indonesia, Bali, Jakarta, Bandung, Yogyakarta, Jogja, Kiaracondong, Lempuyangan, Borobudur, Prambanan, Thailand, Bangkok, Vietnam, Hanoi, Singapore, Malaysia, Kuala Lumpur, Dubai, Nepal, Kathmandu, Sri Lanka, Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, airport, visa, passport, hostel, homestay, scooter, Gojek, Grab, street food, budget, itinerary, rupiah, dollar

## 09 · Outros and CTAs (friendly, smiling)
1. Video pasand aaya toh like karo, share karo, aur channel ko subscribe karna mat bhoolna!
2. Milte hain agle safar mein. Tab tak, ghoomte raho!
3. Aur haan, agla video isse bhi mazedaar hone wala hai.
4. Follow karo aur aise hi aur travel reels ke liye jude raho!

## 10 · Free talk (2–3 minutes, no script)
Talk naturally about your favourite trip, as if you're telling a friend. Don't worry about mistakes. This part captures your real conversational voice, which is the most valuable part.
